The Problem: Flash Flood Surges in Mountain Basins

Operational Breakdown

CRITICAL Why Current Systems Fail in Hilly Regions

1. Sub-30 Minute Lead Times

Cloudbursts trigger instantaneous water surges down steep gradients. Traditional downstream river gauges detect spikes only after the flash flood has already formed.

2. Single-Source Forecast Blindspots

Standard weather apps only measure atmospheric rainfall, ignoring crucial physical parameters: terrain slope (DEM), soil moisture saturation, and catchment shape.

3. No Automated Downstream Relocation

Current authorities broadcast generic SMS alerts without knowing which specific villages are in the flood path or where rescue teams should be dispatched.

Our Solution: Multi-Source Predictive Hydro-Intelligence

Data Fusion Architecture

INPUTS Multi-Source Ingestion Engine

🛰️ Atmospheric Precipitation Data

Pulls live IMD Doppler radar data, NASA GPM (Global Precipitation Measurement), and INSAT-3D cloud-top infrared feeds.

⛰️ High-Resolution Geospatial DEM

Processes SRTM 30m Digital Elevation Models to calculate slope angles, watershed basins, and flow accumulation channels.

🌱 Antecedent Soil Saturation

Integrates satellite soil moisture metrics (SMAP/Sentinel-1) to evaluate how much rainwater the soil can absorb before 100% surface runoff occurs.

Multi-Temporal Satellite Radar & Hydrological Ingestion

OUTPUTS Predictive Engine & Dynamic Red Zones

1. Runoff & Surge Forecasting

Calculates hydrograph peak timing and estimated water surge arrival time using a SCS-CN runoff curve model.

2. Dynamic Hazard Red-Zoning

Categorizes contour polygons into Red (Immediate Evac), Amber (Standby), and Green (Shelter Zone).

3. Direct ResQNet Dispatch Hook

Instantly pushes geofenced evacuation orders to responder mobile apps and control centers before water hits.
